Help & answers

Before you install SS AI.

Important details about features, permissions, compatibility and release availability.

What is SS AI ASSISTANT?

An Android AI assistant focused on AI conversations, voice-led help, image understanding and supported phone workflows.

Are all 22 capability areas available right now?

No. This page includes product capability areas and advanced integrations. Check the installed app and official release notes for features currently shipped.

What are the 17 Android action suites?

They group action categories such as calls, messaging, media, device settings, screen context, reminders, files, research and connected devices. Individual actions depend on implementation and permissions.

Will every feature work on every Android phone?

No. Compatibility can vary by Android version, phone manufacturer, hardware, permissions, battery settings and connected services.

How long is the free trial?

The trial allowance will be shown in the app when the trial service is enabled. This page does not promise an unconfirmed duration.

Can I download the official APK now?

The public download control is disabled until a signed production APK, version details and checksum are ready.

Can I buy an SS AI licence here?

Not yet. Secure checkout, payment verification and licence activation need to be connected and tested first.

Why does SS AI ask for Android permissions?

Some features need specific Android access. Review each system prompt and grant only what is needed. You can review app permissions in Android settings.

How is my information handled?

Read the Privacy & Safety notice for information about the app and connected services. Avoid entering passwords, OTPs or payment details into AI conversations.

Where can I read the terms?

See the Terms & Availability and Privacy & Safety pages.
