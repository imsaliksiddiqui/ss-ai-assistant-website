Questions before you start?

Check app availability, access, permissions and release details.

What is SS AI ASSISTANT?

An Android AI assistant focused on AI chat, voice-led help, image understanding and supported phone actions, with additional capability areas being developed or evaluated.

Are all 22 capability areas available now?

No. The catalogue includes core experiences described on the current website as well as roadmap or integration-dependent areas. Confirm availability in the installed app and official release notes.

How many Android actions are included?

This page groups the product into 17 action suites. It does not claim a verified number of working actions. Only actions tested and shipped in the official release should be counted as available.

Will every feature work on every phone?

No. Compatibility depends on Android version, device maker, hardware, permissions, battery settings, connected apps and network access.

How long is the free trial?

The intended free trial is 15 minutes total. Server-side trial enforcement must be verified before it is advertised as an active guarantee.

Can I download SS AI now?

The public download remains disabled until an approved signed production APK, version details and checksum are published.

Can I buy a licence now?

Not on this website yet. Secure checkout, payment verification, persistent licence records and activation need to be implemented and tested first.

What permissions may be needed?

Permission needs depend on the feature you use. Review each Android permission prompt and the Privacy & Safety notice before granting access.

How is my information handled?

Read the Privacy & Safety notice for the currently inspected backend and provider disclosures. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details or other highly sensitive information in AI prompts.
