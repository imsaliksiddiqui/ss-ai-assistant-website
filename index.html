Questions, answered.

Important details about access, payments, and availability.

What is SS AI ASSISTANT?

SS AI ASSISTANT is an AI assistant app intended to help with questions, voice-led interaction, supported phone actions, ideas, and everyday productivity.

How long is the free trial?

The planned free trial is 15 minutes. The app's server-side trial enforcement has not yet been verified as implemented, so the website does not represent it as an active guarantee until that integration is complete.

Can I buy a licence on this website today?

Not yet. Secure checkout, payment verification, licence issuance, and activation must be connected and tested first. No payment is collected on this page.

What is Buy Direct?

Buy Direct is a planned direct purchase path. Its exact product, pricing, and checkout destination must be confirmed before the button can be activated.

When can I download the app?

The public download will be enabled after an approved signed release is available and its version and checksum are published. A CI Debug build is not a public production release.

Will all features work on every phone?

Not necessarily. Capabilities can depend on Android version, hardware, permissions, network access, and service configuration. Review permission prompts carefully and verify important AI-generated information.
