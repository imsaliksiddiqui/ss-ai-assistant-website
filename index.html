Help centre

Questions, answered.

Know what is available, what is planned and what to check before using the app.

What is SS AI ASSISTANT?

An Android AI assistant focused on AI chat, voice-led help, image understanding and supported phone actions, with additional capability areas being developed or evaluated.

Are all the features on this page available now?

No. This catalogue includes current website-described capabilities and roadmap or integration-dependent ideas. Check the official release notes and in-app settings for features actually available in your version.

Will every feature work on every Android phone?

No. Compatibility can depend on Android version, manufacturer, hardware, permissions, battery settings, connected apps and network access.

How long is the free trial?

The planned trial is 15 minutes total. Server-side trial enforcement must be verified before the trial is advertised as an active guarantee.

Can I download the app now?

The public download remains disabled until an approved signed production release is available with its version and checksum.

Can I buy a licence now?

Not on this website yet. Checkout, payment verification, persistent licence records and activation must be completed and tested first.

What permissions does the app need?

Permission needs depend on the feature you use. Review each Android permission prompt and the Privacy & Safety page before granting access.

How is my information handled?

Read the Privacy & Safety notice for the currently inspected backend and provider disclosures. Do not submit passwords, payment details or other highly sensitive information in AI prompts.

Where can I find the terms?

See Terms & Availability for current pre-release information about compatibility, trial, downloads and purchases.
